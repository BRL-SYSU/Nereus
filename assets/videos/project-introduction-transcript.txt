Nereus connects fine-grained underwater attributes with spatially grounded counting.

A dataset and evaluation resource for asking not only what is present, but what the image supports.

Underwater scenes combine clutter, camouflage and densely overlapping organisms.

A category name does not describe morphology, condition or interaction.

A total count does not explain where organisms gather or how their surroundings relate to that pattern.

The manuscript reports 88,555 images and approximately 2.68 million question-answer pairs.

The schema spans 136 attribute dimensions. Fields are organism-specific, not universal to every object.

Heterogeneous underwater sources are organized into object-centered and point-grounded supervision.

Object crops, image context and biological priors support multi-model attribute discussion and arbitration.

Point annotations support region counts, visibility labels, density maps and evidence-linked questions.

Annotation combines model-assisted generation with targeted human review and expert calibration.

Quality ratings and selected-case arbitration are not a whole-dataset accuracy estimate.
