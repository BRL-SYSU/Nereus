Nereus Dataset Notice

Nereus is a derived, multi-source underwater vision and instruction dataset.
It contains Nereus-authored annotations, metadata, and question-answer data
together with third-party visual and annotation material.

IOCfish5K
----------

The grounded_counting component is derived from IOCfish5K, introduced in:

Indiscernible Object Counting in Underwater Scenes
Guolei Sun, Zhaochong An, Yun Liu, Ce Liu, Christos Sakaridis,
Deng-Ping Fan, and Luc Van Gool
IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition, 2023

Official project:
https://github.com/GuoleiSun/Indiscernible-Object-Counting

The IOCfish5K and IOCfish5K-D datasets are released by their authors under
CC BY-NC 4.0. The original authors retain rights in the source images and
annotations. Nereus does not remove or replace their attribution requirements.
This upstream scope includes the original `train_id.txt`, `val_id.txt`, and
`test_id.txt` manifests redistributed under
`grounded_counting/metadata/splits/iocfish5k_original/`.

FathomNet
---------

The object_attribute_understanding component includes records whose image UUID,
source URL, contributor information, annotations, and file hash correspond to
FathomNet Database records.

FathomNet is operated by the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI).
FathomNet visual content remains copyrighted by its applicable owners and is
made available under owner-selected terms. The Nereus root license does not
